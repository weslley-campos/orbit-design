# Orbit Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Weslley Campos ("Orbit", "we", "us") handles personal data in the Orbit app for Android, iOS, desktop, and web (the "App"). It was written to comply with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD"). It should be read together with the Terms of Use.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Weslley Campos, the developer of Orbit. Contact for any privacy matter, including requests to exercise your rights, and contact of the person in charge of data protection (encarregado): **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**

## 2. Data we collect

**Data you give us**

- **Account data:** email address and password. Passwords are never stored in readable form.
- **Google Sign-In data:** if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier from Google. We never receive your Google password.
- **Family data:** the family you create or join, its members, and invitations you send or receive.
- **Financial information you enter:** expenses, income, budgets, savings goals, projections, account names and balances, categories, notes, and card details you register, such as a nickname, the issuer, limit, closing and due dates, and the last digits. This information is entered manually by you or your family members.

**Data collected automatically**

- **Technical data:** IP address, device type, operating system, app version, language, and date and time of access. We keep these access records as required by Brazilian law.
- **Data stored on your device:** your session and preferences (for example "Remember me", theme, and language) are stored locally and encrypted with a key protected by your platform's secure storage (Android Keystore, Apple Keychain, the operating system keyring on desktop, or the browser's Web Crypto API on web).

**Data we do not collect**

- We do **not** connect to your bank or card issuer and do **not** collect bank credentials.
- We do **not** ask for or store full card numbers, security codes (CVV), or card passwords. Do not enter them in free-text fields.
- We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.
- The App does **not** include advertising or third-party analytics and tracking tools.

## 3. How we use your data and the legal bases

- **Create and authenticate your account.** Data: account and Google Sign-In data. Legal basis: performance of a contract (LGPD, art. 7, V).
- **Provide the App's features**: record, calculate, and display your finances, and sync them across your devices. Data: financial information and family data. Legal basis: performance of a contract (art. 7, V).
- **Share family information with your family members.** Data: family data and financial information marked as shared. Legal basis: performance of a contract (art. 7, V).
- **Keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix errors.** Data: technical data. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).
- **Keep access records.** Data: IP address, date and time of access. Legal basis: legal obligation (art. 7, II), under the Marco Civil da Internet, art. 15.
- **Send service messages**, such as password resets, security alerts, and changes to our terms. Data: email address. Legal basis: performance of a contract (art. 7, V).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 4. Sharing with your family members

Orbit is a personal expense manager that you can also use with family members. When you do:

- information added to a family, or marked as shared, is visible to the other members of that family, who may also edit or delete it;
- information in your individual accounts is visible only to you, unless you choose to share it;
- other members can see your name, email address, and profile picture.

If you leave a family, shared records you contributed may remain available to the remaining members as part of the family's history.

## 5. Who else we share data with

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share it only with:

- **Service providers** that operate on our behalf and under our instructions, such as cloud hosting, database, authentication, and email delivery providers;
- **Google**, when you choose to sign in with your Google account, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy);
- **Authorities**, when required by law, court order, or a request from a competent authority;
- **A successor**, if the App is transferred to another person or company, in which case you will be notified beforehand.

## 6. International transfers

Our service providers may store and process data on servers located outside Brazil. When that happens, we rely on the transfer mechanisms provided by the LGPD (art. 33), such as standard contractual clauses, so that your data keeps an adequate level of protection.

## 7. How long we keep your data

- **Account, family, and financial data:** while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize this data within 30 days, except for shared family records that remain with the remaining members (see section 4) and data we are required to keep by law.
- **Access records:** 6 months, as required by the Marco Civil da Internet.
- **Backups:** deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 additional days before being overwritten.
- **Data on your device:** until you sign out, clear the App's data, or uninstall it.

## 8. How we protect your data

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of financial information, including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption of the data stored on your device, hashed passwords, and access restricted to what is needed to operate the App.

No system is completely secure. If a security incident creates relevant risk or harm to you, we will notify you and the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) as required by law.

## 9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you may at any time:

- confirm whether we process your data and access it;
- correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
- request anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data;
- request portability of your data;
- request deletion of data processed with your consent, and withdraw consent;
- learn who we shared your data with;
- object to processing that does not comply with the law.

To exercise your rights, write to **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**. We respond within 15 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. You also have the right to file a complaint with the ANPD ([gov.br/anpd](https://www.gov.br/anpd)).

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have equivalent rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 10. Children and teenagers

Orbit is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
